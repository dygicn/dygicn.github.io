About Me

I am an academic and researcher working at the intersection of data mining, multivariate data analysis, and big data analytics. My research focuses on regression trees, fuzzy regression modeling, and machine learning methods, with an emphasis on developing interpretable and robust approaches for complex real-world data.

Since 2018, I teach undergraduate and postgraduate courses in these areas, combining theoretical foundations with practical applications. I am particularly interested in research collaborations that integrate applied statistics, machine learning, and interdisciplinary problem solving.

Beyond academia, I have a strong interest in sailing and regularly participate in national and international races.
